Licensing

What the licensing authority will accept

London (TfL)

Every PHV licensed for the first time must be zero emission capable: fully electric, or a plug-in hybrid with low enough emissions and enough electric range. That rules out diesel V-Class, E 220 d and S 350 d as new licences. PHVs can be re-licensed up to 10 years old.

Greater Manchester and Leeds

Most Greater Manchester councils require Euro 4 petrol, Euro 6 diesel or zero emission capable cars by 31 December 2026. Leeds executive PHVs must be on its approved list, under 1 year old when first licensed and no older than 5.

Clean air zones

Birmingham and Bradford charge non-compliant vehicles daily, and Edinburgh’s low emission zone has been enforced since June 2024. In London the Congestion Charge is £18 a day, with 25% off for electric cars on Auto Pay.
