Questions drivers ask

Straight answers

Will the car be accepted by Uber, Wheely or Blacklane?

We check the car against each platform’s published rules for model, age, colour and equipment before you commit, and tell you in writing what we checked. Platforms change their rules and some, like Blacklane, publish model lists only in their partner portals, so please confirm with the platform as well.

Can I license the car with a council instead of, or as well as, TfL?

Yes, many chauffeurs do. You can hold driver licences with more than one authority, but each car is normally licensed by one: Hertsmere, for example, refuses cars licensed elsewhere. Each council sets its own rules on vehicle age, emissions and plate exemptions, and the driver, car and operator for a booking need to be licensed by the same authority. Tell us your council and we’ll match the car to its policy. Always check with the council before you buy.

Can a diesel V-Class or S-Class be licensed in London?

Not for the first time. Since 1 January 2023 TfL only licenses new PHVs that are zero emission capable. A diesel car that already holds a London PHV licence can be re-licensed until it reaches 10 years old.

Do you sell new S-Class cars?

Yes. We source new and nearly new S-Class. New gives you the longest life on platforms with age limits; nearly new saves the first-year drop. We’ll compare both on the call.

I don’t drive myself. I rent cars to chauffeurs. Can you help?

Yes. We supply businesses that rent exec cars to drivers, and we can source several cars to the same spec.

Will you take my current car?

Yes, as a part-exchange or a straight purchase, including cars close to their platform age limit. We can settle outstanding finance as part of the deal.
