Why buy now, and buy right

Three rules that decide which car earns

Uber’s 2026 rules

New rules apply to cars added from 1 August 2026 in London and 19 October 2026 elsewhere. Exec takes Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Jaguar and Lexus saloons up to 5 model years old in London, Manchester and Birmingham, 6 elsewhere, in dark colours in the big three cities.

London’s zero emission rule

Since 2023 every car licensed as a London PHV for the first time must be zero emission capable. Plug-in hybrids like the S 580 e, E 300 e and 750e qualify, as do EQE, EQS, EQV and i7. A diesel V-Class or S 350 d does not.

Nearly new is where the value is

Big luxury EVs lose value fast. In October 2026 a 2023 EQS 450+ was listed at about £45,000 against a new price from £91,765, and 2024 i7s from under £50,000 against £104,230 new.

The car, and the payout, should be yours

Questions drivers ask

Straight answers

Will the car be accepted by Uber, Wheely or Blacklane?

We check the car against each platform’s published rules for model, age, colour and equipment before you commit, and tell you in writing what we checked. Platforms change their rules and some, like Blacklane, publish model lists only in their partner portals, so please confirm with the platform as well.

Can a diesel V-Class or S-Class be licensed in London?

Not for the first time. Since 1 January 2023 TfL only licenses new PHVs that are zero emission capable. A diesel car that already holds a London PHV licence can be re-licensed until it reaches 10 years old. For a new London licence choose an EQV, EQE, EQS, i7, or a plug-in hybrid such as the S 580 e, E 300 e or 750e.

Do you sell new cars?

We specialise in nearly new cars, usually one to three years old, where the price drop from new is largest. If you need a new car, ask and we’ll tell you what we can source.

Will you take my current car?

Yes, as a part-exchange or a straight purchase, including cars close to their platform age limit. Have the registration, mileage and service history ready for the call.

What happens on the call?

A specialist rings at the time you chose. We talk through your work, the cars that fit, current prices and a rough value for your car. There’s no obligation.
