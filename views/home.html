Why now

Three things changed for London cabbies in 2026

New TX supply has stopped

Final assembly of the current TX finished in September 2026. The replacement is planned for the second half of 2027. Until then, a good used TX is the only way into a new-generation London cab.

The taxi grant has gone

The Plug-in Taxi Grant closed in April 2026, so there is no government money off a new electric cab. A well-chosen used TX closes that gap.

The 15-year clock keeps running

TfL will not relicense a taxi past 15 years from first registration, whatever its condition. If your TX4 or Vito is getting close, you need a plan before the plate runs out.

Questions drivers ask

Straight answers

Can I still buy a brand new LEVC TX?

Final assembly of the current TX ended in September 2026, so new stock is limited to whatever remains with dealers. LEVC plans a new model in the second half of 2027. A used TX is the practical option for most drivers until then.

Will parts and servicing still be available for the TX?

LEVC has said existing TX owners will keep receiving parts, servicing and technical support through its aftersales operations after assembly ends.

Is there still a grant for electric taxis?

No. The Plug-in Taxi Grant closed in April 2026. Any price you see now is without a government grant.

Can a used TX be licensed in London?

Yes. TfL requires newly licensed taxis to be zero emission capable, which the TX is, and limits London taxis to 15 years from first registration. We check a cab's age and history so you know how many working years it has left.

Will you take my TX4 or Vito?

Yes, as a part-exchange or a straight purchase, including cabs close to their London age limit. Book a call and have your registration and mileage ready.
