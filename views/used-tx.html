Choosing a used TX

What to look at

We talk you through all of this on the call. Here's the short version.

Year and plate life

A London taxi can be licensed for up to 15 years from first registration. The earlier the cab, the fewer working years you get, so we price that in and tell you exactly how long it has left.

Trim and options

TX taxis were sold in Icon and the higher Vista trim, with option packs such as Comfort Plus. Most drivers wanted Vista. Tell us what matters to you and we'll filter for it.

Mileage and battery

The TX runs on battery for most London work and uses its petrol range extender for longer runs. We check charging, electric range on test and the service record on every cab we offer.

Why drivers move to the TX

Quieter shifts, lower running costs

Electric running in town means less spent on fuel than a diesel TX4, and the TX is zero emission capable, which TfL requires for any newly licensed London taxi. Six passengers, a wheelchair ramp, a tight turning circle and rapid charging come as standard.

Your actual savings depend on how and where you charge and how many miles you do. We'll go through the numbers for your shifts on the call.
