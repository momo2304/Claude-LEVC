# Reverie Executive: sales call playbook

For the team taking booked calls from chauffeurs and exec drivers. They picked the time, so ring **on the minute**. Many are between jobs, so keep it tight.

## Before the call (2 minutes)

1. Open `/admin`. Check the **hot / warm / nurture** tag and their answers: car, main work, licence, trading as, budget, current car and year, timeframe, spec notes.
2. Run their car and year through the **eligibility checker** on the site, for both the car they want and the car they drive now.
3. Have matching stock or sourcing options open (dealer, auction, trade).

## The call (10–15 minutes)

**Open**
> "Hi [name], it's [you] from Reverie Executive, part of Reverie Autos. You booked a call about [an EQS / an S-Class for Wheely First]. Is now still good, or are you on a job?"

If they're busy: offer two times today or tomorrow.

**Qualify**
- "Where does most of your work come from: Uber, Wheely, Blacklane, your own clients?"
- "Are you licensed with TfL or a council? Which city do you work in?" (London new licences must be zero emission capable.)
- "What are you driving now and what year? Is it still getting the work you want?"
- "Do you trade as a sole trader or a limited company?" (Routes finance. Don't quote finance unless it's switched on and you're authorised.)
- "What budget are you working to?"
- "Any must-haves: colour, long wheelbase, panoramic roof, rear blinds, tinted windows?"

**Match the platform rules (facts to use)**
- Uber Exec: Mercedes/BMW/Audi/Jaguar/Lexus saloon, 5 model years in London, Manchester, Birmingham (6 elsewhere), dark colours in those cities. New rules for cars added from 19 Oct 2026 outside London.
- Wheely Business: E-Class W214, EQE, EQS, 7/i7 G70, 3 years old or newer; EQE/EQS black or graphite grey; BMW tinted windows.
- Wheely First: S-Class W223 long wheelbase, 2024+, panoramic roof, rear blinds, reclining heated rear seats, rear climate.
- Wheely XL: V-Class/EQV long or extra-long, 3 years or newer, child seat.
- London first licence: zero emission capable only. EQV not diesel V-Class; S 580 e not S 350 d; E 300 e not E 220 d; 750e not 740d.

**Council-licensed drivers**
- Ask which council licenses their car (Hertsmere, Windsor & Maidenhead, Castle Point, other).
- Hertsmere: up to 6 years old at first licence, Euro 6, no zero emission rule; won't license a car licensed elsewhere.
- RBWM: up to 5 years at first licence; plate exemption needs written business contracts; Wheely needs the exemption.
- Wheely accepts these councils, so a diesel S 350 d L / S 400 d L can do Wheely First. Always tell them to confirm with the council and Wheely.

**Always offer the S-Class comparison**
- New vs nearly new S-Class (longest platform life vs first-year saving), and S 580 e vs diesel depending on licence.

**Rental businesses (no driver licence)**
- How many cars, which platforms their drivers work, colours/specs, replacement cycle, how they buy (Ltd company).

**Agree one next step**
- Shortlist of matching cars by WhatsApp/email by an agreed time, or
- Viewing / collection appointment at Welwyn Garden City, or
- Valuation of their current car (reg, mileage, photos, service history, finance settlement), or
- Follow-up date if they're 3+ months out.

## After the call

Set the outcome in `/admin` and add notes:

| Status | Use when |
|---|---|
| Called | Spoke, no clear outcome yet |
| No answer | Retry in 15 minutes, then once later the same day; send a text |
| Qualified | Need, budget, timeframe and spec confirmed; next step agreed |
| Won | Deposit taken / deal agreed |
| Lost | Add the reason (bought elsewhere, renting instead, not eligible, price) |

## Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I'll just rent-to-buy." | "That's an option. Check who owns the car and who gets the insurance payout if it's written off. With us the car is registered to you from day one." |
| "EVs lose value too fast." | "That's why nearly new makes sense. The first owner took the biggest drop. A 2023 EQS was listed around £45k this month against over £91k new." |
| "Will Uber/Wheely accept it?" | "We check model, year, colour and spec against their published rules and put it in writing. Please confirm with the platform too, because they change the rules." |
| "My car still has a year left." | "Let's look now so you're not without a car when it ages out. I'll book a follow-up for [date]." |
| "Can I use a diesel V-Class in London?" | "Only if it already has a London PHV licence. For a new licence it has to be the EQV." |

## Rules

- Never promise platform acceptance; we check against published rules and the driver confirms.
- Never quote finance rates or monthly payments unless finance is switched on and you are following the approved process (including commission disclosure).
- Don't confirm a part-exchange value until the car is inspected.
- Only send stock alerts to people who ticked marketing consent.
