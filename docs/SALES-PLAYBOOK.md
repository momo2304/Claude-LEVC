# Sales playbook: booked calls

For the Reverie Cab Sales team. Each call is booked by the driver at a time they chose, so ring **on the minute**.

## Before the call (2 minutes)

1. Open `/admin`. Today's calls are at the top, with a **hot / warm / nurture** tag.
2. Read what they told us: what they want, licence, current cab and year, payment, timeframe, notes.
3. If they have a cab 12+ years old, work out roughly when its London licence ends (first registration year + 15).
4. Have current TX stock or sourcing options open.

## The call (aim for 10–15 minutes)

**Open**
> "Hi [name], it's [you] from Reverie Cab Sales, part of Reverie Autos. You booked a call with us for now about [a used TX / part-exchanging your TX4]. Is now still OK?"

If not OK: offer two times today or tomorrow, then update the booking or ask them to use the link in their email.

**Confirm and qualify**
- "You're [green / yellow badge], driving a [TX4] from [year], is that right?"
- "When does your plate run out?" (part-exchange) / "When do you want to be in the new cab?"
- "What spec matters to you? Vista or Icon, colour, mileage?"
- "How are you thinking of paying?" (Only discuss finance if finance is enabled on the site and you are authorised to.)
- "What's your budget roughly?"

**Explain the situation (facts only)**
- New TX assembly finished in September 2026; LEVC's next cab is due in the second half of 2027.
- The Plug-in Taxi Grant closed in April 2026.
- LEVC says parts, servicing and support continue for the TX.
- TfL will not relicense a taxi past 15 years.

**Next step (always agree one)**
- Viewing appointment (Hertfordshire, appointment only), or
- We send matching cabs by WhatsApp/email by an agreed day, or
- Valuation: ask for reg, mileage, photos, V5C first registration date, or
- Planned follow-up date if they're 6+ months out.

## After the call

In `/admin`, set the outcome and write notes in the same row:

| Status | Use when |
|---|---|
| Called | Spoke, no clear outcome yet |
| No answer | Didn't pick up. Try again within 15 minutes, then once later that day; send a text. |
| Qualified | Real need, budget and timeframe; next step agreed |
| Won | Deposit taken / deal agreed |
| Lost | Not buying, bought elsewhere, not eligible. Add the reason. |

## Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I'll wait for the new LEVC." | "It's due in the second half of 2027 and prices aren't out yet. How long is left on your plate? If it runs out before then, a used TX keeps you working." |
| "What about parts if they've stopped making it?" | "LEVC has said parts, servicing and support carry on for TX owners." |
| "Used TXs are too dear." | "Let's look at what your current cab is worth against it, and what you'd save running electric on your shifts." |
| "I'm not ready yet." | "No problem. When does your plate run out? I'll put a call in for [date] and look out for the right cab before then." |

## Rules

- Never quote finance rates or monthly payments unless the firm is FCA authorised for it and you are following its process.
- Don't promise a valuation until we've seen the cab.
- Only use marketing contact (new stock alerts) for drivers who ticked that box.
